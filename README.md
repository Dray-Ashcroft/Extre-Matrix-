Xtrematrix Prejudix

Interactive Natural Selection & Evolution Simulator

Xtrematrix Prejudix is an interactive visualization exploring biological variation, environmental selection pressures, survival advantages, and evolutionary adaptation through simulated populations.

🧬 Variation Simulation

🌱 Environmental Interaction
Explore the relationship between organisms and their surroundings through interactive evolution scenarios.
